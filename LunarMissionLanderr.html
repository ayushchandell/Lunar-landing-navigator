Polar regions

The Moon's South Pole

Some spots get sunlight almost all the time. Others never see the Sun.

Peaks of Eternal Light

Some ridge tops are sunlit for much of the lunar month, often cited around 80% of the time. This makes them useful for solar power.

Permanently Shadowed Regions

Deep crater floors never see sunlight. Some areas drop below −200 °C, which may trap water ice.

Recent Landings

Chandrayaan-3 (2023) and Intuitive Machines' IM-1 Odysseus (2024) both landed in the south polar region.

Solar power

Sunlight Efficiency

The Moon has no air, so sunlight arrives strong and unfiltered: about 1,361 watts per square metre. Try the calculator to estimate how much power a panel could make.
